Privacy Policy

How OneVictoria collects, uses, and protects resident data under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Effective 1 January 2026 Updated 22 July 2026 RA 10173

1Introduction & scope

OneVictoria is the official digital Resident Hub of the Municipality of Victoria, Tarlac. This policy covers account registration, events, volunteer applications, donations, and related notices.

Legal basis. Processing is carried out under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its IRR, and applicable ordinances of Victoria, Tarlac.

By using OneVictoria you confirm that you have read and understood this policy.

2Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to deliver municipal services.

Identification

Name, email, mobile number, age, gender, and residential address.

Service data

Volunteer skills, availability, supporting files, service hours, and event registrations.

Donations

Amount, purpose, payment method reference, and donor identification.

Technical logs

IP address, browser, device type, and platform interaction logs.

3How we use your information

Data is processed only for legitimate municipal purposes:

  • Account management — create and secure your resident account and verify identity.
  • Community services — process events, volunteer applications, and service hours.
  • Donation processing — record gifts, issue references, and keep transparent logs. Names appear publicly only with consent.
  • Notices — send critical updates, announcements, and policy changes.
  • Platform improvement — debug issues, review aggregated trends, and strengthen security.
  • Legal compliance — meet statutory duties and lawful government requests.

4Sharing & disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information for commercial purposes.
  • Municipal departments — authorized LGU staff who need the data to deliver the service.
  • Service providers — vendors bound by contract to protect your information.
  • Legal process — when required by law or to protect rights and public safety.
  • Public acknowledgment — donor names are published only with explicit consent; otherwise donations stay anonymous.

5Security measures

We apply administrative, technical, and physical safeguards:

Encryption SSL/TLS in transit
Access control Role-limited servers
Monitoring Audits and updates
No system is 100% secure. We keep improving controls to match current industry practice. Protect your password and never share it.

6Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, or as Philippine law requires.

  • Active accounts — retained while the profile remains active.
  • Deactivation — upon a valid request we securely dispose of data within 30–60 days, subject to legal holds.
  • Legal archives — some records may be kept for audit, finance, or statutory retention.

7Your rights

Under RA 10173 you may exercise the following. Contact the DPO in Section 9.

Informed

Know how your data is processed.

Access

Request a copy of data we hold.

Rectification

Correct inaccurate or incomplete records.

Object

Withdraw consent for specific uses.

Erasure / blocking

Request deletion where the law allows.

Complaint

File with the National Privacy Commission.

8Cookies & tracking

We use essential cookies only — to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and protect the session.

We do not use advertising cookies, track you across other sites, or sell browsing data. Disabling essential cookies may stop login and payment confirmation from working.

10Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy when our practices, systems, or the law change. When we do, we will:

  • Notify registered residents in the OneVictoria notification center
  • Post the new version with an updated effective date
  • Summarize material changes where appropriate

Continued use after an update takes effect means you accept the revised policy.
