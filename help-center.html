Getting started

OneVictoria is the official resident hub of the Municipality of Victoria, Tarlac. After you sign in you can:

  • Read announcements and register for events
  • Apply as a volunteer and track service hours
  • Donate money or items
  • Receive notifications about approvals and updates
  1. On the homepage, choose Create Account.
  2. Enter name, email, phone (09XXXXXXXXX), age (15+), gender, address, and a password of at least 6 characters.
  3. Submit, then open the verification email before you sign in.
Use an email you can access. Unverified accounts cannot log in.
  1. Enter your registered email and password.
  2. Select Login.
If the email is not verified, you will be signed out until you click the link in your inbox.

Account

  1. Open Profile Settings from your name or photo.
  2. Choose Edit Profile.
  3. Change mobile number, address, or photo, then Save Changes.
Name, email, gender, and age are locked. Ask support if those must change.
  1. Profile Settings → Edit Profile.
  2. Type a new password (6+ characters) and save.

Events

  1. News & Events → Events.
  2. Open Register / Join on a card and confirm.
  1. Open My Events.
  2. Choose Cancel Registration and confirm.
Cancel at least 24 hours before the event when you can.
Registered Completed Cancelled

Registered — you are on the list. Completed — attendance was recorded. Cancelled — you or the organizer removed the booking.

Volunteering

  1. Community → Volunteer.
  2. Choose skill, experience, and availability.
  3. Upload proof of skill, then Submit Application.
Staff review applications. You will get a notification when the status changes.

Maximum 5MB. Accepted: PDF, JPG/PNG, DOC/DOCX — certificates, licenses, or reference letters.

Service hours

Hours are credited when you attend a registered event and an organizer certifies your participation. Totals appear under Service Hours.

Approved Pending

Approved counts toward your total. Pending is waiting for organizer review.

Donations

Money: Community → Donations → amount and purpose → Donate → choose a payment method.

Item: switch to Item, describe the goods and purpose, then submit. Staff will arrange pickup or drop-off. No payment is needed.

  • GCash, PayMaya, Bank Transfer — Pay Now shows a QR / account details. Pay in the app, then upload a screenshot and submit.
  • Cash — Pay Now asks for your account password. Confirm, then pay in person at Municipal Hall (Mon–Fri, 8:00 AM–5:00 PM).
Donations stay Pending until municipal staff verify them. They are generally non-refundable.

Notifications

Use the bell in the header for a preview, or See All Notifications for the full list.

Open a notification to mark it read, or choose Mark all read. Clear permanently deletes them.

Troubleshooting

  1. On the login panel, open Forgot Password?
  2. Enter your registered email and send the reset link.
  3. Check inbox and spam, then set a new password.
The email must already have an account. If nothing arrives in a few minutes, contact support.
  • Refresh (F5 or Ctrl/Cmd+R)
  • Clear cache and cookies
  • Try Chrome, Firefox, or Edge with JavaScript on
  • Check your connection

Reviews usually take 1–3 business days. You will get a notification. If it has been longer, contact support with your registered email.

OneVictoria allows one active session per account. Signing in on another phone or browser ends the previous session.
